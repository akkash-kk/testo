Questions people ask

What is SequelBox?

An online 3D mockup editor. Upload your artwork and preview it on clothing, packaging, phones, laptops and more.

What is Motion Box?

Our online video editor. Cut clips, add captions, music and transitions, and export a finished video from your browser.

Do I need design or 3D skills?

No. Pick a template, add your artwork and adjust colors and size.

What can I mock up?

Dresses, T-shirts, hoodies, caps, phones, laptops, tablets, boxes, pouches, bottles and more. New templates are added every month.

What can I export?

PNG and transparent PNG images, 360° turntables and MP4 videos from Motion Box.

Can I send a mockup to Motion Box?

Yes. Export your mockup and open it in Motion Box to build a video around it.

Can I use the results commercially?

Yes, commercial use is included on paid plans.

Can I upload my own 3D model?

Yes, on the Studio plan.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. The SequelBox app and the website share the same account and projects.
