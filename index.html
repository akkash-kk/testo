FAQ

What is SequelBox?

SequelBox is an online studio for 3D product mockups. Upload your artwork and preview it on clothing, packaging, phones, laptops and more.

What is Motion Box?

Motion Box is our online video editor. Trim, caption and score footage in your browser and export a finished video.

Do I need 3D or design skills?

No. Pick a template, add your artwork and adjust colors and size.

Which products can I mock up?

Dresses, T-shirts, hoodies, caps, phones, laptops, tablets, boxes, pouches, bottles and more, with new templates added regularly.

Can I send a mockup to Motion Box?

Yes. Export your mockup and open it in Motion Box to build a video around it.

Does SequelBox have an API?

Yes. The API lets you render mockups from your own system.

Can I use SequelBox for free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free, with a few exports every month.

Can teams share projects?

Yes. Team plans include a shared workspace, libraries and review links.
